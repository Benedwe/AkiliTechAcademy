CLEAR ANSWERS FOR PARENTS

Before your child gets started.

Check the common questions below, or send an enquiry and we can discuss what will suit your family.

Ask us directly ↗
At what age can children start learning?+

Many children can begin with age-appropriate computer basics from around age seven. We’ll help match the starting point to your child’s interests and comfort.

Does the tutor provide home visits?+

Home tutoring may be available in Dar es Salaam, subject to location and schedule. Share your area in the enquiry form to check.

Are computers provided?+

Equipment arrangements depend on the course and location. Please mention what you have available when you get in touch. Hardware lessons focus on safe, beginner-level care and troubleshooting.

How long does each course take?+

Course lengths vary. The course cards include a guide; we can recommend a suitable plan after learning about your goals.

Are courses suitable for complete beginners?+

Yes. Lessons are planned to be approachable, practical and paced for the learner.

Are holiday training packages available?+

Holiday programs can be arranged. Contact us to ask about the next available dates.

Which areas of Dar es Salaam are covered?+

Coverage depends on availability. Tell us your neighborhood and we’ll confirm whether a home visit is possible.

How can parents register their children?+

Complete the enquiry form below to open a pre-filled WhatsApp message, or contact us by phone or email.
